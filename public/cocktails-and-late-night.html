Bar-forward, restaurant too

Cocktails &
Late Nights

The Balboa is built bar-first, with a kitchen running beside it: a long bar, a cocktail program led by Tony Contreras, and a full on-premises liquor license application that would keep the room open late, every day. per Bridgetown Bites and WhatNow Portland

The bar program

Long bar, slow drinks

“I think if people walk in and the first thing they see is this big oven, it really screams restaurant more so than a bar/lounge kind of vibe.”Sam Smith, chef — Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

That line explains the whole approach: the kitchen is there to feed people, but the room is built around the bar. Tony Contreras — the bartender at Tusk, who brought Sam onto this project — leads the cocktail program. The drinks list is still being finalized, and prices come with it. What the team has talked about: great cocktails, a proper beer option, and two NA options built into the cocktail list — including a non-alcoholic avocado margarita, the drink Tony is the most excited about. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

On the application

How late, how loud

Applied-for hours

Every day, noon to 2:30 a.m., per the full on-premises liquor license application. Final hours will be announced at opening. per WhatNow Portland

The sound

Recorded music on a proper sound system, with DJs from time to time in the works — the application notes no live music and no karaoke. per WhatNow Portland and Bridgetown Bites

Dancing

Dancing is indicated on the liquor license application, with a pool table under the windows for the in-between. per WhatNow Portland and Bridgetown Bites

Age

Aimed primarily at a 21-and-over crowd — probably not so much a spot for minors, per the team. per Bridgetown Bites

After work, downtown

A happy hour for the people still at their desks

“I want it to be a spot that people want to come to after work.”Sam Smith, chef — Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

A happy-hour menu is planned with downtown’s after-work crowd in mind — the office blocks around Wieden+Kennedy are a short walk away. The team expects half the room to come for a drink and half to come for a full meal, and the menu is built to serve both. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026
