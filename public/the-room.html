The Room

A big open room on NW Everett, being rebuilt around the bar: booths down both sides of a central path, a long bar, a pool table under the windows, darker tones, low light, candles. It is the same building the Pearl has known — and it won’t feel the same. per Bridgetown Bites and WhatNow Portland

“Definitely, it’s going to feel very, very different.”Sam Smith, chef — on the old room, to Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026
What changes and what stays

Booths, a long bar, and one pool table

Booths, not banquettes

The long bench seating from the former tenant is coming out — larger booths down one side, two-person booths on the other, and a pathway down the middle. per Bridgetown Bites

The long bar stays

The bar stays super long, and the pool table goes over toward the windows where the old seating sat. per Bridgetown Bites

Seats & patio

The license application puts 75 dining seats inside with 16 more on an outdoor patio; the team says the final count is still open and could land near 120. per WhatNow Portland and Bridgetown Bites

The wood oven stays

Ripping out the huge wood oven wasn’t worth the work — it stays, with a partition built around it so the room, not the kitchen, is the feature. per Bridgetown Bites

Low light, darker tones

The team is aiming for darker tones, low lights and candles — a room meant for the evening. per Bridgetown Bites

The night mix

Recorded music on a proper sound system, DJs in the works, dancing on the application, pool on the side — and no live music or karaoke on the current application. per WhatNow Portland and Bridgetown Bites

The neighborhood

The busy end of the Pearl

The address sits in a mixed-use block near NW 12th Avenue, the stretch of the Pearl that has been getting busier at night — one block from where Kurt Huffman co-opened Pearl Tavern at 11th and Everett in 2016. The team says the energy downtown — the new spots opening around NW 12th and the West Burnside — is a big part of why they chose this corner. per WhatNow Portland and Bridgetown Bites

Between NW 11th and 12th, near Everett — the former Oven & Shaker building.
