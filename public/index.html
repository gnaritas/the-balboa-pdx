The Balboa

A bar-forward restaurant at 1134 NW Everett Street — classic American cooking from the 1940s and ’60s out of a huge wood oven, cocktails built for lingering, and a room made for music, dancing and one long bar with a pool table under the windows.

What’s coming

A bar first. A restaurant, too.

The team is building the old Oven & Shaker room into something darker and looser — booths down the sides, a long bar, a pool table by the windows, low light, candles, and a kitchen running a wood oven. The concept chef Sam Smith describes as bar-forward, with a restaurant alongside it. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Cocktails & late nights

Tony Contreras — the bartender at Tusk — leads the bar program. The group has applied for a full on-premises liquor license, with daily service from noon to 2:30 a.m. on the application.

Where the name comes from

Named for a jazz club

The name is Tony Contreras’s family history: the Balboa Club, a live-jazz hall his great-grandfather owned in Empire, Oregon in the 1940s. It burned down decades ago. This room is what the team is building in its spirit. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026
