Straight answers, with sources

Questions, answered

The Balboa is still in build-out, so the honest answers are short. Everything below comes from the team’s own words in interviews and from the liquor license application filed with the state.

Ask us something elseRead the story first
When are you opening?

Announced for 2026, with the opening date still to be announced. Leave your email at the contact desk and you’ll get the news the day it’s set. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Is this the old Oven & Shaker space?

Yes — 1134 NW Everett Street, the building the Pearl knows. It is not a continuation of Oven & Shaker (which closed at the end of October 2026): the team is calling it a restaurant first, with the bar as the main feature, and the room is being rebuilt around booths, a long bar and a pool table. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

What kind of food will you serve?

Classic American cuisine from the 1940s to the 1960s — chopped salad, spinach-artichoke dip, roast chicken from the wood oven, shrimp scampi, a crab salad club sandwich, a five-ounce burger, Clams Casino, Salisbury steak frites. The final menu and prices are still in the works. per chef Sam Smith to Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Are you doing pizza?

Probably not. The question comes up a lot because of the huge wood oven and the former tenant — chef Sam Smith’s answer: “I don’t think so.” per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Will there be live music?

Not live music or karaoke on the current license application. The room runs recorded music on a proper sound system, with DJs from time to time in the works — and dancing is indicated on the application. There’s also a pool table under the windows. per WhatNow Portland and Bridgetown Bites

What are your hours?

Not set yet. The full on-premises liquor license application asks for daily service from noon to 2:30 a.m.; final hours will be announced at opening. per WhatNow Portland

Are kids welcome?

It’s aimed primarily at a 21-and-over crowd — the team says it’s probably not so much a spot for minors. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Will you have a happy hour?

Yes — a happy-hour menu is planned, aimed at the downtown after-work crowd. Details come with the opening. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Where does the name come from?

From the Balboa Club, a live-jazz hall bartender Tony Contreras’s great-grandfather owned in Empire, Oregon in the 1940s. The team is building this room in its spirit. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Is the wood oven still there?

Yes — ripping it out wasn’t worth the work. It stays, with a partition built around it so the bar-forward room is the feature; a roast chicken and a rotating seasonal vegetable are planned for it. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Question not on the list?

The management desk answers every note — menu, events, press, opening news.
