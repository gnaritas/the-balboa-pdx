Menu direction · still in final form

Classic American, 1940s–60s

Chef Sam Smith describes the menu as classic American cuisine from the 1940s to the 1960s — nostalgic, generous, farm-driven sides, and a lot of it cooked in the room’s huge wood oven. The dishes below are the ones Sam has talked about; the final menu and prices land closer to opening. per Bridgetown Bites, Nov 16, 2026

Every dish above is described by chef Sam Smith as intended for the menu, in Bridgetown Bites on November 16, 2026 — the menu is still being finalized. And the question everyone asks about the wood oven: pizza? Sam’s answer, same interview: “I don’t think so.”
