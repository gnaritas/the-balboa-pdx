The short version

Privacy, plainly

This site collects as little as possible. What you send us stays with us.

Contact the deskBack to the front page

What we keep

When you fill in the contact form, we receive your name, email, phone (if you left one) and your message. That’s it. The information is used to answer you — and, if you asked for opening news, to email you when the date is set. It’s kept by The Balboa’s management team and not shared, sold, or used for advertising.

The map image on this site is served by Google Maps, which sees your IP address while the map loads. Your web host also logs routine technical details (IP address, browser type) to keep the site running.

What we don’t do

No advertising networks. No tracking scripts. No analytics cookies. No third-party pixels. This site does not follow you around the internet, and nothing you type here is used to build a profile about you.

To see or remove anything we hold from you, email info@chefstablegroup.com or call 503-222-0600 and say the word.
